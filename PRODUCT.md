# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience is B2B technical and operations buyers: asset managers and O&M leads at wind and solar operators, O&M contractors, energy development companies, and utilities across Centro-Sud Italia. Not private/residential customers — the prior site's residential-solar framing is explicitly off-positioning here. These visitors often arrive under time pressure (an active fault, a compliance deadline) and need to answer one question fast: can this company put hands on a 150/30 kV substation and arrive the same day. The job to be done is a rapid credibility check that ends in a phone call.

## Product Purpose

A static B2B showcase site for Amon Energy S.r.l., a Puglia-based specialist in AT/MT/BT electrical infrastructure and renewable energy plants (founded 2020). It replaces an outdated WordPress site whose residential-solar copy undermines B2B authority. There is no lead-capture form by design — the site exists to move a qualified visitor to a direct call or email, not to collect submissions. Success is a technical decision-maker reading the site in about 10 seconds, believing the company can handle serious AT/MT work, and calling.

## Positioning

"Professionisti dell'energia" — the team that builds, verifies, and manages Centro-Sud Italia's energy infrastructure end to end, from construction through ongoing O&M, with rapid on-site response as the differentiator.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: phone call (`tel:`). Secondary CTA: email (`mailto:`) — no generic contact form anywhere in the site.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: these people can put hands on a 150/30 kV substation and arrive today.
- Belief ladder: real technical specialists, evidenced by cited norms and real instrumentation → fast Centro-Sud coverage, evidenced by HQ location and response time → proven, verifiable work, evidenced by named projects with kV/client data → call now.
- Proof on hand: a named client roster (E-Distribuzione, EDPR, Tozzi Sud, STE Energy, Fortore Energia, Poligrafico e Zecca dello Stato, CROSSENERGY, Eco Puglia Energia); six documented substation projects with kV rating, location, and client (Troia, Banzi–Montemilone, Deliceto, EPE Troia, Aquilonia, Castelluccio); 20+ years of team experience (the one verified stat). Other headline metrics (installations managed, MW monitored, interventions/year) are placeholder values pending client-confirmed numbers and must stay marked as such in code until replaced.

## Brand Personality

Technical, direct, authoritative. No motivational fluff — short factual sentences, correct sector terminology (CEI 0-16, ARERA, MT/AT, SSE) used as a trust signal rather than jargon for its own sake. Visually this is "industrial precision", rendered as **one navy brand** across the whole site: deep navy surfaces everywhere, Archivo Expanded display type, real substation photography. The **control world** (Control Room, RCS, CCI, 24/7 monitoring) is still a distinct *voice* drawn from the company presentation — a Source Serif 4 authority face, telemetry panels, live-telemetry motion — but it is no longer a distinct *colour*: the forest-green drench was retired because it read as a second brand. The A-leaf monogram and the leaf-green accent are the through-line, kept to small marks only. The site should read like a serious electrical-engineering contractor's collateral, not a SaaS startup's landing page.

## Anti-references

The current WordPress site and its residential-solar copy — off-positioning, dilutes authority. SaaS-startup visual tropes: glassmorphism, saturated gradients, hero-metric templates. Generic stock photography of "smiling guys in hardhats." Gallery clichés explicitly ruled out for the projects page: Pinterest-style masonry, uniform square thumbnail grids, autoplay carousels, 50%-black overlay with centered title, flip/tilt hover effects.

## Design Principles

Proof over adjectives — real client names and real project kV/location data beat any "industry leader" superlative. One idea per section — no walls of text; each section carries a single message. Technical specificity is the trust signal — citing real norms and real instrumentation is the badge of authority for this audience, not decoration. Friction-free conversion — every page ends in a direct call/email CTA, with phone as the primary channel because the buying trigger is often an active fault. Speed as a design value, not just a metric — the site should feel as fast as the emergency response it promises.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected everywhere, disabling the animated stat counters and the home hero's background video — which is then not even downloaded, only its poster frame. Touch targets are at least 44×44px, critical for the tel/mail buttons. No information is conveyed by hover alone — gallery captions and metadata stay visible by default rather than hover-only, since touch has no hover state.
